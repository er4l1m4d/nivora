# Qestia — YouTube Listing

## Title
Qestia — Turn Your Notes Into a Staked Quiz That Pays You to Show Up

## Description
Studying is usually solo and forgettable. Qestia turns your lecture notes into a live, staked quiz where everyone puts in a stake and the top three split the pot — and everyone who finishes gets 80% back. Under quorum, you're refunded in full. It literally pays to show up.

In this demo:
- Upload a real lecture **PDF** and let the **AI draft the whole Qest** (cloze, multiple choice, true-or-false).
- Commit a stake, fill the room, and go **LIVE** — one answer per question, locked the moment you confirm.
- Answers unseal, the podium lands, and every question comes with an explanation.

Built with a Nimiq feeless payments layer so commitments verify on-chain.
