# Qestia — Narration (teleprompter)

Word-for-word, ~140 wpm, ~2:45 total. Eight beats. Each beat is one take.

---

**[0:00 — Hook]** (title card on screen)
What if your notes paid you for showing up? I built Qestia — a quiz app where studying actually has skin in the game.

**[0:05 — Problem]**
Online studying is solo, and there's no skin in the game. Qestia flips that — you commit a stake, and the room keeps you honest.

**[0:15 — Create: PDF → AI]** (upload PDF, watch AI draft)
Here's the new part. Upload a real lecture PDF — and Qestia's AI reads it and drafts the whole Qest for you. Cloze, multiple choice, true-or-false. You review one question, tweak it, set the stake and the start time, and publish. You never see the answers, so you play blind, just like everyone else.

**[0:55 — Commit]** (both windows commit)
Now the commitment. Each player locks in the stake — verified on-chain with a personal memo code in real mode, instant in this demo. The room fills, the quorum meter ticks up.

**[1:10 — Compete]** (answer live)
Quorum met — the room goes live. One answer per question, locked the moment you confirm. The clock is the server's, not yours. Wrong answers and no-shows feed the pool; finishing pays a bonus.

**[1:45 — Settle / Results]** (podium + explanations)
When everyone's done, answers unseal. Ties share a rank's cut, and every payout is money-conserving — the pot always adds up. And every question opens with an explanation — that's the actually-learning part.

**[2:15 — Economics]** (economics card)
The economics: finish and get 80% back. Top three split the rest — 50, 30, 10. Under quorum, everyone's refunded in full. It literally pays to show up.

**[2:30 — CTA]** (thumbnail / welcome)
Qestia. Turn today's lecture into tonight's Qest. Link in the description. Know it. Prove it.

---

**Teleprompter notes**
- Pause ½ beat after each bold on-screen moment; let the UI breathe.
- "Cloze" — say it plainly: "close-the-gap questions."
- Keep energy up on the Compete beat; slow down on Economics so the numbers land.
